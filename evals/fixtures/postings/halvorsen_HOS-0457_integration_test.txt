Spacecraft Integration and Test Engineer II
Halvorsen Orbital Systems - Denver, CO (onsite)

About the role
Halvorsen Orbital Systems builds small satellite buses for government and
commercial customers. Our Integration and Test (I&T) team takes flight units
from first power-on through environmental testing and launch site support.

Key Responsibilities:

- Write and execute integration and test procedures for spacecraft avionics
  and subsystems
- Perform electrical integration of flight harnesses, including continuity,
  isolation, and pin-to-pin checks
- Support environmental testing including thermal vacuum (TVAC) and vibration
- Develop automated test scripts in Python to command units and log telemetry
- Troubleshoot anomalies, document nonconformances, and drive corrective action
- Work with ground support equipment (GSE) and electrostatic discharge (ESD)
  controls in a cleanroom

Basic Qualifications:

- Bachelor of Science in Electrical, Aerospace, or Computer Engineering AND 2
  years of related experience
- Hands-on experience with oscilloscopes, digital multimeters, and power
  supplies
- Experience with thermal vacuum (TVAC) testing of flight or flight-like
  hardware
- Experience writing test procedures
- U.S. citizenship and the ability to obtain and maintain a DoD Secret
  clearance

Preferred Qualifications:

- Experience with Python test automation
- Experience with LabVIEW
- Flight harness fabrication or J-STD-001 certification
- Experience with spacecraft command and telemetry databases
- Launch site support experience
