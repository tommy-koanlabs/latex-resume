Principal / Senior Principal Mechanical Engineer - Test Equipment Design
Calderwick Aerospace Systems - Huntsville, AL (onsite)

Description
Calderwick Aerospace Systems builds propulsion and fluid control hardware for
launch vehicles and spacecraft. Our Test and Evaluation team designs and
operates the test stations that prove that hardware before it flies. We are
looking for a Principal / Senior Principal Mechanical Engineer to design the
next generation of those test stations and the laboratory systems around them.

This requisition may be filled at the Principal OR Senior Principal level based
on the qualifications listed below.

In this role you will take test equipment from a blank page to a station that
technicians run every day. You will own the mechanical design, the drawing
package, and the bill of materials, and you will stay with the build through
fabrication, integration, and acceptance testing.

Key Responsibilities:

- Design test stations, test fixtures, and laboratory systems from concept
  through fabrication, integration, and checkout
- Produce solid models, assemblies, and detailed engineering drawings in
  Siemens NX (or an equivalent 3D CAD tool), applying GD&T per ASME Y14.5
- Create and maintain Bills of Materials (BOMs) and release design
  documentation through Teamcenter PLM under configuration management
- Develop and execute Acceptance Test Procedures (ATPs) for new and modified
  test stations
- Integrate instrumentation and data acquisition (DAQ) hardware into test
  equipment
- Design pneumatic and fluid systems for test stations in accordance with
  ASME B31.3
- Perform structural analysis of fixtures and support structure
- Coordinate with a multi-discipline team (supply chain, operations, quality
  assurance, manufacturing) to keep builds on schedule and within budget
- Provide manufacturing liaison support during fabrication, assembly, and
  installation
- Mentor less experienced engineers and help document design best practices

Basic Qualifications (Principal):

- Bachelor of Science degree in a STEM discipline AND 5 years of related
  experience, OR a Master of Science degree in a STEM discipline AND 3 years
  of related experience
- Experience with Siemens NX or another 3D mechanical design tool
- Hands-on experience with test equipment such as oscilloscopes, power
  supplies, and multimeters
- Working knowledge of GD&T
- Understanding of test engineering concepts
- U.S. citizenship and the ability to obtain and maintain a DoD Secret
  clearance

Basic Qualifications (Senior Principal):

- Bachelor of Science degree in a STEM discipline AND 8 years of related
  experience, OR a Master of Science degree in a STEM discipline AND 6 years
  of related experience
- All other Principal-level basic qualifications

Preferred Qualifications:

- MS in Mechanical or Aerospace Engineering
- Experience with test station design and development
- Experience creating and maintaining BOMs
- Experience developing and executing ATPs
- Experience with Teamcenter
- Experience with LabVIEW
- Manufacturing liaison experience supporting build and installation of
  assemblies
- Active DoD Secret clearance

We offer a 9/80 work schedule with every other Friday off, relocation
