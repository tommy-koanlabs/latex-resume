Senior Structural Analysis Engineer
Brannock Aerostructures - Huntsville, AL (hybrid)

Description
Brannock Aerostructures designs and qualifies primary and secondary structure
for missile and launch systems. The Stress and Loads group performs the
analysis that proves our structure is safe to fly and test. We are looking for
a Senior Structural Analysis Engineer to own stress analysis of metallic
structure, fixtures, and ground support equipment from preliminary design
through test correlation.

Key Responsibilities:

- Perform stress analysis of metallic structures, brackets, and ground support
  equipment using finite element analysis (FEA) and classical hand
  calculations
- Build and run linear static, modal, and buckling finite element models in
  MSC Nastran; pre- and post-process in Patran or HyperMesh
- Calculate margins of safety and document results in formal stress reports
  for internal and customer review
- Perform fatigue and fracture mechanics analysis of welded and bolted joints
- Perform bolted joint analysis, including preload and separation checks
- Plan strain gauge instrumentation and correlate test data with analysis
  predictions
- Support design engineers with load path and sizing recommendations during
  design reviews
- Automate repetitive analysis tasks with Python or MATLAB

Basic Qualifications:

- Bachelor of Science in Mechanical, Aerospace, or Civil Engineering AND 5
  years of related experience, OR a Master of Science AND 3 years
- Experience with finite element analysis using MSC Nastran, ANSYS, or
  Abaqus
- Experience writing stress reports with margins of safety
- Working knowledge of fatigue analysis
- U.S. citizenship and the ability to obtain and maintain a DoD Secret
  clearance

Preferred Qualifications:

- MS in Mechanical or Aerospace Engineering
- Experience with HyperMesh or Patran
- Experience with fracture mechanics tools such as NASGRO
- Experience analyzing composite structures
- Experience correlating analysis with strain gauge test data
- Programming experience in Python or MATLAB
- Active DoD Secret clearance
